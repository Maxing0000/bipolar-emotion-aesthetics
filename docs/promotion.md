### 2. 短介绍（用于仓库 About、模型卡片、社群推荐）

**中：**
为什么有的设计一眼高级，有的却甜腻廉价？双极情绪美学（BEA）把美感拆解为「亲极 P+」（圆润、柔和、安全）与「危极 T−」（尖锐、对比、刺激）两极，核心命题是「美感=可控张力下的情绪奖赏」。

BEA 提供：元素级极性谱系、三级阈值体系、W(T) 危极指数计算、六大范式配比、BEA 四维评分卡、六步创作法、病症—处方诊断表。适用于产品、汽车、建筑、品牌、UI、服装、声音、影视等一切感官形式。

开源免费（CC BY 4.0），可直接作为 AI Agent Skill 调用。

**EN:**
Why do some designs feel instantly sophisticated while others look cloying and cheap? Bipolar Emotion Aesthetics (BEA) decomposes beauty into two poles: **Pole of Attraction (P+)** — round, soft, safe — and **Pole of Threat (T−)** — sharp, contrastive, stimulating. Its core thesis: **aesthetic pleasure = emotional reward under controlled tension**.

BEA provides: an element-level polarity spectrum, a three-tier threshold system, the W(T) threat index, six archetype ratios, a four-dimensional BEA scorecard, a six-step creation method, and a symptom–prescription diagnosis table. Applicable to products, cars, architecture, branding, UI, fashion, sound, film — any sensory form.

Open source and free (CC BY 4.0), directly usable as an AI Agent Skill.

### 3. 长介绍（用于扣子上架、知乎/小红书/Medium 文章开头）

**中：**
我们每天都在判断"好不好看"，但很少有人能说清"为什么好看"。设计师靠感觉，甲方靠偏好，AI 绘图靠运气——审美似乎是最不可量化的事。

双极情绪美学（Bipolar Emotion Aesthetics, BEA）试图改变这一点。它的起点很朴素：一切形式元素都先天携带两种情绪极性——圆润、柔色、光滑让人想接近（亲极 P+），尖锐、强对比、突变让人警觉（危极 T−）。纯亲极甜腻平庸，纯危极攻击排斥；只有当双极在可认知的秩序中组合、且危极被约束在安全阈值内，警觉才被"收编"为快感，产生「既被吸引、又被安抚」的复合愉悦。

BEA 把这套逻辑工程化：你可以给每个元素标 0–10 的极性强度，算出整体危极指数 W(T)，对照六大范式配比（治愈松弛 90:10 / 亲和精致 80:20 / 均衡典雅 60:40 / 崇高震撼 45:55 / 冷峻克制 38:62 / 先锋反叛 30:70），用四维评分卡打分，再对照病症表（甜腻症/空洞症/攻击症/均分症/失序症…）拿到元素级修改处方。

它不是玄学，也不是要取代审美直觉——它是把直觉变成可沟通、可复现、可迭代的工作语言。无论是设计师评审方案、AI 提示词工程师控制画面张力，还是普通人想搞懂"为什么这张海报高级"，BEA 都提供了一套上手即用的工具。

**EN:**
We judge "is it beautiful" every day, yet rarely can we explain *why*. Designers rely on feel, clients on preference, AI image generators on luck — aesthetics seems the least quantifiable thing there is.

Bipolar Emotion Aesthetics (BEA) attempts to change that. Its starting point is simple: every formal element innately carries one of two emotional polarities — round, soft, smooth forms make us want to approach (Pole of Attraction, P+); sharp, high-contrast, sudden forms make us vigilant (Pole of Threat, T−). Pure P+ feels cloying and bland; pure T− feels aggressive and repulsive. Only when both poles are composed within cognizable order, and the threat-pole is kept within the safety threshold, is vigilance "recruited" into pleasure — yielding the compound joy of being *captivated yet soothed*.

BEA engineers this logic: you can rate each element's polarity on a 0–10 scale, compute the overall W(T) threat index, map it against six archetype ratios, score with a four-dimensional scorecard, and retrieve element-level prescriptions from a symptom table. It is not mysticism, nor does it replace aesthetic intuition — it turns intuition into a communicable, reproducible, iterable working language.
