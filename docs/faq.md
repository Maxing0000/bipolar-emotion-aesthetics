## Q1. BEA 是一门新科学吗？/ Is BEA a new science?

**中：** BEA 不是基础科学新发现，而是一套**工程化的应用美学框架**。它的底层思想（神经美学、崇高理论、格式塔、唤醒理论等）来自已有的公共学术知识；BEA 的原创性在于把这些思想整合为一套**元素级、可计算、可诊断、可指导创作**的操作工具。它更像"设计领域的会计科目表"——不发明数学，但让审美决策可沟通、可复现。

**EN:** BEA is not a new basic science, but an **engineering-style applied aesthetics framework**. Its underlying ideas (neuroaesthetics, the sublime, Gestalt, arousal theory) come from established public scholarship. BEA's originality lies in integrating them into an **element-level, computable, diagnosable, creation-guiding toolkit**. It is more like "a chart of accounts for design" — it doesn't invent math, but it makes aesthetic decisions communicable and reproducible.

## Q2. BEA 的分数是客观的吗？/ Are BEA scores objective?

**中：** 不是绝对客观真值。W(T) 危极指数和 BEA 评分卡是**相对协作量表**，价值在于统一团队对"多锐算锐、多乱算乱"的刻度，把审美争论转化为变量调整。不同评审者对同一对象可能有 ±5–10 分的主观区间，应以**趋势与相对排序**为决策依据，而非绝对分数。BEA 不宣称自己是"科学客观测量"。

**EN:** No, they are not absolute objective truths. W(T) and the BEA scorecard are **relative collaborative scales**; their value is aligning a team on "how sharp is sharp, how chaotic is chaotic," turning aesthetic debates into variable adjustments. Different reviewers may show a ±5–10 subjective range; decisions should rely on **trends and relative ranking**, not absolute scores. BEA does not claim to be "scientific objective measurement."

## Q3. BEA 和康德的崇高美学有什么区别？/ How does BEA differ from Kant's sublime?

**中：** 伯克/康德区分了"优美"与"崇高"——优美是柔和愉悦，崇高是"先恐惧后愉悦"。BEA 把这两种范畴**降维为同一连续谱上的不同配比**：优美≈亲极主导（治愈/亲和范式），崇高≈危极高强度但被秩序与安全框架接住（崇高震撼范式 45:55）。区别在于：① BEA 是**元素级可操作**的，不是哲学描述；② BEA 增加了**三级阈值**来解释"什么时候崇高变成恐惧"；③ BEA 覆盖全部形式领域，不限于自然与艺术静观。

**EN:** Burke/Kant distinguished the *beautiful* from the *sublime* — the beautiful is soft pleasure, the sublime is "fear followed by pleasure." BEA **reduces both categories to different ratios on one continuous spectrum**: the beautiful ≈ attraction-dominant (Healing / Approachable archetypes), the sublime ≈ high-intensity threat held by order and a safety frame (Sublime archetype 45:55). Differences: (1) BEA is **element-level and actionable**, not philosophical description; (2) BEA adds a **three-tier threshold** to explain "when the sublime becomes terror"; (3) BEA covers all formal domains, not just nature and art contemplation.

## Q6. 极性会随文化变化吗？BEA 是普适的吗？/ Does polarity change across cultures? Is BEA universal?

**中：** 分层回答。极性**方向**（圆润=安全、尖锐=警觉）有跨文化生理恒常性，这是 BEA 普遍性的根基；但极性**强度**与**符号联想**随文化、时代变化，甚至符号层局部翻转（如红色在中国=喜庆，在部分西方语境=警告）。BEA 的做法是：**生理层通用，符号层本地化**——跨文化设计必须重做符号层极性审计。所以 BEA 既不是"绝对普适"，也不是"纯主观"，而是普遍层 + 相对层的分层模型。

**EN:** Layered answer. Polarity **direction** (round = safe, sharp = alert) has cross-cultural physiological constancy — the foundation of BEA's universality. But polarity **intensity** and **symbolic association** vary with culture and era, and may even reverse at the symbol layer (e.g., red = celebration in China, warning in some Western contexts). BEA's approach: **the physiological layer is universal, the symbol layer is localized** — cross-cultural design must redo the symbol-layer polarity audit. So BEA is neither "absolutely universal" nor "purely subjective"; it is a layered model of universal + relative.

**EN:** Suited for any object carried by sensory form: product design, cars, architecture/interiors, brand/graphics, UI, fashion, sound, film, installation. Not suited for: content/narrative, moral judgment, engineering safety/performance, medical assessment, legal compliance — these have their own professional standards, and BEA scores cannot endorse them. See [boundary.md](boundary.md).

## Q8. BEA 和"黄金比例""格式塔"这类经典设计理论是什么关系？/ How does BEA relate to golden ratio, Gestalt, etc.?

**中：** 兼容且互补。黄金比例、对称、栅格等是**秩序手段**，在 BEA 中归入"秩序"维度，负责消化张力；格式塔是 BEA"秩序如何起作用"的心理学机制；但这些经典理论只回答"怎么整齐"，不回答"为什么整齐却可能无聊"、"为什么尖锐却可能高级"。BEA 用**双极张力 + 阈值**补上了另一半：秩序是骨架，张力是灵魂，阈值是边界。

**EN:** Compatible and complementary. Golden ratio, symmetry, grids are **order techniques** — in BEA they belong to the "Order" dimension, responsible for digesting tension. Gestalt is the psychological mechanism of how BEA's "order" works. But these classic theories only answer "how to be orderly," not "why orderly can be boring" or "why sharp can be sophisticated." BEA adds the other half with **bipolar tension + threshold**: order is the skeleton, tension is the soul, threshold is the boundary.
